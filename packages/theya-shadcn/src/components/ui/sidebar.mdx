# Sidebar

App navigation people switch between all day: grouped destinations, counts and per-item actions. Collapses to an icon rail and becomes a slide-over panel on a phone.

Sidebar is a family of parts: wrap your app in `SidebarProvider`, then render `Sidebar` next to
your main content and fill it from the parts below. The rail is 224px wide, 64px collapsed, and
remembers its state in a cookie.

This page documents every part's own props — the autodocs table on a single-component page
only covers one component, and Sidebar is a family of nine.

## SidebarProvider

Wraps the rail and your main content. Owns collapsed/mobile-open state and exposes it via
`useSidebar()`.

## Sidebar

The rail itself (renders both the desktop `<aside>` and the mobile `Drawer` slide-over).

## SidebarHeader

The top row — usually `SidebarBrand` + `SidebarCollapse`.

<ArgTypes of={SidebarHeader} />

## SidebarBrand

The logo mark + product name. When the rail is collapsed, this doubles as the "Expand sidebar"
control (the only way out of a collapsed rail — WCAG 2.1.1).

<ArgTypes of={SidebarBrand} />

## SidebarCollapse

The collapse toggle. Hidden automatically while already collapsed (the brand mark takes over).

<ArgTypes of={SidebarCollapse} />

## SidebarNav

The scrollable nav landmark that holds one or more `SidebarSection`s.

<ArgTypes of={SidebarNav} />

## SidebarSection

A labelled group of items, with an optional trailing `action` next to the label (e.g. an
"Add" button).

<ArgTypes of={SidebarSection} />

## SidebarItem

A single nav link — icon, label, an optional tone-able `badge`, and an optional hover-revealed
`actions` menu.
