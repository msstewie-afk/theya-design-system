Landing patterns (beta): scroll behaviours for cards — StickyStack (cards pile up as the page scrolls, plain position: sticky), CardRail (a Carousel row of cards with the next one peeking in, arrows at the top end) and StickySteps (a sticky heading and numbered step links beside scrolling steps, the step in view marked with aria-current="step"). Plus a RevealGroup story for cards that ease in. No scroll hijacking, nothing on a timer. StoryCard's media area gets one height so neighbouring cards line up.
