Landing `Stamp` (`blocks/landing/stamp`): a circle or rounded-square badge on a pastel accent fill with a dashed inner border and a tilt (−15…+15°), one to three lines in the heavy display face, `ring` for words around the edge, fluid sizes sm / md / lg. Decorative by default (`aria-hidden`); `label` makes it an image with that name. LANDING rule: on a phone it covers no more than one letter of a word.
