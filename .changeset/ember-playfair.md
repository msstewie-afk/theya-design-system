Ember's heading face is Playfair Display (was EB Garamond), self-hosted in `@theya/tokens/fonts` (variable 400–900, normal + italic; Latin, Cyrillic, Vietnamese). `.font-heading` sets lining figures, so a serif heading face's old-style figures don't bob along the line ("10 weeks", "115 kcal"); Geologica's figures are lining already. EB Garamond stays the landing display serif.
