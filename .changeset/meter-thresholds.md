Meter: `thresholds` prop. Each `{ value, label?, tone? }` draws a tick on the bar and a label under it; once the value reaches a threshold the fill takes its tone (warning by default), and the aria text names the range and the nearest thresholds ("1.1 g of 3 g, below the 2 g threshold"). Strings are localized (en, ru, de, ar, zh).
