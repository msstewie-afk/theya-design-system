ImageGround: a picture as a section's ground with a theme-colored tint (`primary`, `deep` — primary with black, tone in tone — `surface`, `scrim`), picture `opacity` (a veil over the panel's fill) and `flip` (x / y / both, optional mirroring in RTL). SectionPanel renders it for `image` and takes its options in `imageGround`; Hero takes it as `backdrop`. LANDING.md: adjust the ground before changing the picture.
