Landing cards part by borders, not shadows. Tonal LandingCards get a translucent border in their own hue (as tonal carousel cards already do); the beam and tilt hovers drop their shadow, beam keeps only its light edge. Demo previews, feature visuals, moving link cards, the Flip card's back (now a translucent primary rim), the folder's front and the testimonial metrics plate lose their shadows; plates over pictures take the subtle border.
