Heavy rounded display: `font-display-heavy` utility. The Latin face comes from the theme (`displayHeavyFont` in the profile): Bowlby One by default and in dusk, Paytone One in ember and ochre, Rammetto One in iris, Fredoka (700) in lagoon and lime. Cyrillic is always Rubik Black, one family per Latin face with `size-adjust` matched to its cap height. All OFL-1.1, self-hosted in @theya/tokens/fonts.
